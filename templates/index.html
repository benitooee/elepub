Why We Adopt, What We Value, and How AI Understands It

Adopting a pet is a deeply personal decision influenced by lifestyle, finances, emotional readiness, and compassion. This study examines how AI models mimic those choices, from budgeting for care to understanding activity needs, helping us uncover how “human” their decision-making really is.
